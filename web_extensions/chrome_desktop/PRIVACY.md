# Privacy Policy for Food Processor

**Effective date:** August 16, 2026

Food Processor is a Chrome extension that extracts recipe information from the webpage you are currently viewing and presents it in a clean, easy-to-read format.

## Information Food Processor Accesses

When you explicitly click the Food Processor extension, it may access:

* Recipe-related content from the currently active webpage, including structured recipe data such as ingredients, instructions, servings, and cooking times.
* The URL of the currently active webpage so the extension can identify the source of the recipe.

Food Processor does not continuously monitor your browsing activity. Access to webpage content occurs only after you invoke the extension.

## How Your Information Is Used

The accessed webpage information is used solely to identify, extract, and display recipe information within the Food Processor extension.

All processing occurs locally in your browser.

## Data Collection and Storage

Food Processor does **not**:

* Send webpage content or browsing information to a server.
* Collect or store personal information.
* Maintain a history of websites you visit.
* Sell, rent, or share user data.
* Use user data for advertising or profiling.
* Use analytics or tracking services.

Recipe information is processed locally while you use the extension and is not retained by Food Processor after it is no longer needed by the extension.

## Chrome Permissions

Food Processor uses the following Chrome permissions:

**activeTab**
Allows Food Processor to temporarily access the webpage you are currently viewing after you explicitly click the extension.

**scripting**
Allows Food Processor to run its recipe extraction code on the active webpage in order to locate and read structured recipe information.

These permissions are used only for the extension's primary purpose of extracting and displaying recipe information.

## Third Parties

Food Processor does not transmit user data to third parties.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Food Processor's functionality or data practices change. Any updated policy will be published at this location.
